HM Echo Privacy Policy

Updated October 16, 2026

HM Echo is a game that plays patterns on your HomeKit lights. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Information that stays on your device

HM Echo does not require an account. It does not send your information to the developer, and it has no advertising or analytics.

The app uses HomeKit, with your permission, to list your homes, rooms, and lights and to turn those lights on and off and set their brightness and color while you play. Home and room names, light names, and the commands that play the game stay in Apple’s HomeKit on your devices. HM Echo does not upload them.

If you save a room, HM Echo stores that setup on your device. A saved setup can include the name you give it, which home and room it uses, which lights are included, brightness, and the on-screen order of those lights. Game settings such as speed and sound are stored on your device as well.

Deletion

You can delete a saved room in Settings. Removing the HM Echo app deletes the setups and settings stored by the app. HomeKit information remains in the Home app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
