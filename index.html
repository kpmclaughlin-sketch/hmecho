How to play

Watch the pattern, then repeat it. Each round adds one more step. You can miss three times. Play it again shows the current pattern once more, and that does not count as a miss. You can replay as often as you like.

Flash Match

Pick two to five dimmable lights. They flash in order. Tap the matching buttons in that same order.

Color Match

Pick one color light. It plays a sequence of colors. Tap the color pads in that same order.

Multi-Match

Pick two to four color lights. Each light has three colors. Tap the wedge that matches the color you just saw.

A color-temperature light does not count as a color light. A HomeKit group counts as one light until you split it into individual bulbs.

Play in a new room to choose a home, a room, and a mode, then check the lights that should play. Start stays off until those lights are on. Save the room if you want to use it again. Playing a saved room asks only for the mode.

Reorganize lights matches a lamp to an on-screen button. Reset lights turns the game lamps back on if one was switched off. Both stay hidden while the pattern is playing. Game UI only plays on the screen, with no HomeKit lights.

Speed and sound are in Settings.

Privacy policy

Updated October 16, 2026

HM Echo is a game that plays patterns on your HomeKit lights. This policy describes how the app handles information.

Information that stays on your device

HM Echo does not require an account. It does not send your information to the developer, and it has no advertising or analytics.

The app uses HomeKit, with your permission, to list your homes, rooms, and lights and to turn those lights on and off and set their brightness and color while you play. Home and room names, light names, and the commands that play the game stay in Apple’s HomeKit on your devices. HM Echo does not upload them.

If you save a room, HM Echo stores that setup on your device. A saved setup can include the name you give it, which home and room it uses, which lights are included, brightness, and the on-screen order of those lights. Game settings such as speed and sound are stored on your device as well.

Deletion

You can delete a saved room in Settings. Removing the HM Echo app deletes the setups and settings stored by the app. HomeKit information remains in the Home app.
